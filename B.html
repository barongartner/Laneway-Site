The problem

Maps route across a graph. Some edges were never in it.

Mapping apps are excellent at what they have data for. They route across digitized road and transit graphs, and anything missing from that graph is simply invisible to them. Private shuttles, terminal interiors, courtesy vans, informal connections: the edges do not exist, so the answer that comes back is technically valid and practically useless.

The anchor case, and the one Laneway is tested against: you land at Toronto Pearson Terminal 1 and need an off-airport rental depot. The shuttle that serves it is in no transit feed, and the walk between terminals is not a mapped footway, so nothing in any graph connects the two.

The third leg is the point. Laneway does not know its timetable, so it does not invent one. It says so, and it still gives you the leg, because "roughly every 20 minutes, call ahead" is a usable answer and a two hour walk is not.

The one rule

Never present a fact the system cannot back up.

Any competent model can produce a plausible airport itinerary. The reason nobody trusts that output is that plausible and correct look identical on the page. One fabricated shuttle stop destroys trust permanently.

So every claim in a Laneway plan is checked against a retrieved source before you see it, and the checker is allowed to say no. Three verdicts, never collapsed into two. These are real results from the engine:

Supported
"MiWay route 7 serves stops on Airport Road."
Checked against MiWay official GTFS schedule feed The feed lists a stop at 6445 Airport Rd
Contradicted
"MiWay route 7 enters Pearson Terminal 1."
Checked against MiWay official GTFS schedule feed Route termini are City Centre and Westwood Square. The claim was dropped.
Unsupported
"The rental shuttle leaves from the arrivals level post."
Checked against Operator website, unreachable Shown to you as unverified, never quietly deleted and never stated as fact.

A fact without provenance is a bug, not a feature, so the type system refuses to build one: inside the engine an unsourced fact is literally unconstructible. Every source carries the URL it came from and the moment it was fetched.

How it works

A slow layer that thinks, a fast layer that checks.

The model proposes. It never gets the last word. Retrieval, caching and composition are deterministic with no model calls inside them, which is what keeps both the cost and the failure modes predictable.

01

Intake

Plain language becomes a structured trip query. When it is ambiguous it asks rather than guesses.

02

Planner

Decides which legs to attempt and what evidence each one needs. Treated as untrusted throughout.

03

Retrieval

Official feeds first, then the open web. Cache-first, robots respected, crawler identified honestly.

04

Composer

Chains legs into a journey and checks the timing is actually possible. Deterministic.

05

Verifier

Every claim traced to a verbatim span in a real source, or marked unverified. Fails closed.

06

Present

Map, timeline, turn-by-turn, and a citation on every leg. Saved plans work with no signal.

Popular routes warm a shared cache, so the hundredth person asking about Pearson to a rental depot gets an answer that is already verified. That is the difference between scraping as pure cost and scraping as a dataset that usage pays for.

Where it actually is

Honest status, since that is the whole product.

Working today

  • Verified multi-modal plans with a source and a fetch time on every claim
  • Turn-by-turn navigation with off-route detection and a lock screen Live Activity
  • Live vehicle positions where the operator publishes a realtime feed
  • Walking routes on real footpaths from OpenStreetMap, not around the block
  • Saved plans that open offline, because airport wifi fails exactly when you need the plan

Not there yet

  • No public release. There is nothing to download yet, and no App Store listing.
  • iOS only, and Canada only, on purpose. Coverage grows one transit feed at a time.
  • No voice guidance yet, and no booking or payment for any individual leg.
  • Some sources block crawlers. When that happens the leg comes back honestly unverified.

The engineering log is public and updated as the work happens. Read the dev log.

What it is becoming

An alternative to Apple and Google Maps whose routes are verified.

Planning and verification are the foundation and the moat. Navigation is the surface you live in. The goal is a maps app you can trust on the leg that matters, the one nothing else will route for you.
